## Personal Information We Collect

In order to maximize your experience and provide our Services, we may process personal information from the categories listed below.

- **Identifiers:** This may include your name, address, email address, telephone number, IP address, cookies and other similar online identifiers.
- **Internet or other electronic network activity:** This may include your browser type, browser identifier, session information, site navigation data, and other similar information.
- **Geolocation data:** This may include your approximate location inferred from IP address or interaction with geotargeted ads.
- **Commercial information:** This may include purchase invoices, records of services purchased and/or considered, payment information, and business contact information.
- **Audio, electronic, visual, thermal, olfactory, or similar information:** This may include audio data gathered during phone calls and photos/videos uploaded by our customers.

## Sources of Personal Information We Collect

**Directly from you.** We collect personal information directly from you when you submit a service request/quote; subscribe to email updates; upload or submit content through the Services; or contact us with inquiries.

**Indirectly from you.** We may automatically collect personal information when you visit the Services through the use of tracking technologies, including:

- **Log Information** — browser type and language, operating system, device information, ISP, date and time of requests.
- **Cookies and Similar Tracking Technologies** — Necessary, Functional, Performance, Customer Interaction, and Advertising/Targeting cookies.

**From third parties.** We may collect personal information about you from technical service partners (hosting, marketing, analytics, advertising) and payment processors.

## How We Use Personal Information

- To provide you information about our Services, including newsletters and marketing materials.
- To communicate with you, including responding to customer service requests.

- To authenticate and verify individual identities.

We do not share your personal information with outside parties except to the extent necessary to provide information to you or as stated in this Privacy Policy.

Residents of certain states have additional rights under applicable privacy laws.

This Notice is provided pursuant to the California Consumer Privacy Act, as modified by the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020 ("CCPA"). It applies solely to consumers who reside in the State of California.

**Selling and Sharing of Personal Information.** NearU does not "sell" or "rent" personal information, nor do we have actual knowledge of any "sale" of personal information of minors under 16 years of age. We may share Identifiers, Commercial Information, and Internet/Electronic Network Activity with advertising partners for targeted advertising. To the extent this is considered a "sale," "sharing," or "targeted advertising" under the CCPA, you may limit such sharing as described below. We never sell, rent, or share your telephone number.

**Retention of Personal Information.** We retain personal information for as long as necessary to manage our operations and relationship with you, carry out a disclosed purpose, protect our rights, or as required by law.

### Additional Information for California Residents

As a California resident, subject to certain exceptions, you have the following rights:

- **Right to Know and Access** — request access to personal information we've collected, used, disclosed, or sold about you.
- **Right to Deletion** — request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain legal exceptions.
- **Right to Correction** — request correction of inaccurate personal information.
- **Right to Opt-out of Sales and Sharing** — opt out of your information being shared for targeted advertising.
- **Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information** — we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information in a way governed by the CCPA.
- **Right to Non-Discrimination** — you will not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

**Exercising Your Rights.** Contact us at [privacy@nearu-services.com](mailto:privacy@nearu-services.com) or call (833) 490-9104. We will attempt to respond within 45 days.
